# Data Deletion Policy

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026

At **The Studio**, we believe your wardrobe belongs to you, your Studio belongs to you, and your data belongs to you.

This Data Deletion Policy explains how you can delete information stored in The Studio and what happens after a deletion request is received.

---

# Deleting Individual Items

You may delete individual items from your Studio at any time.

This includes, but is not limited to:

- Clothing
- Shoes
- Handbags
- Accessories
- Fragrances
- Photos
- Saved Looks
- Favorites
- Wish List items
- Notes

Once an item is deleted, it is removed from your active Studio.

---

# Members Using Local Storage Only

You may enjoy The Studio without enabling **Keep My Studio**.

If you have not enabled Keep My Studio, your Studio is stored locally on your device.

Removing the application from your device generally removes locally stored application data, subject to iOS system behavior.

---

# Members Using Keep My Studio

If you choose to enable **Keep My Studio**, your Studio may be securely backed up to support:

- Secure cloud backup
- Syncing across supported devices
- Restoring your Studio after replacing a device

If you later decide to delete your Studio, you may request that your cloud-stored Studio information also be removed.

---

# What Is Deleted

When a deletion request is completed, we will remove the information associated with your Studio, including:

- Wardrobe items
- Photos
- Saved Looks
- Collections
- Favorites
- Wish List
- Fragrance Library
- Related Studio information associated with your account

---

# Processing Time

We process deletion requests as promptly as reasonably possible.

Some encrypted backups or disaster recovery systems may require additional time before deleted information is permanently removed.

---

# Legal Exceptions

In limited circumstances, we may retain certain information where required to:

- Comply with applicable law
- Protect against fraud or abuse
- Resolve disputes
- Enforce legal agreements
- Maintain the security and integrity of our services

Any retained information will be limited to what is reasonably necessary for those purposes.

---

# Your Control

You remain in control of your Studio.

You may:

- Delete individual items at any time.
- Delete your entire Studio.
- Contact us with questions about deleting your information.

---

# Contact Us

If you have questions about this Data Deletion Policy or would like assistance with deleting your Studio, please contact us:

**privacy@thestudiowardrobe.app**

---

Thank you for trusting The Studio with your wardrobe.

We are committed to handling your information with care, transparency, and respect.
