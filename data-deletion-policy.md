Data Deletion Policy
Effective Date: August 1, 2026
Your Studio belongs to you.
You remain in control of the information you choose to store in The Studio.
Deleting Individual Items
You may delete individual clothing items, photographs, collections, Looks, fragrances, wish list entries, and other content at any time.
Once deleted, the information is removed from your active Studio.
Local-Only Members
If you choose not to enable Keep My Studio, your information remains stored locally on your device.
Removing the application generally removes locally stored application data, subject to iOS system behavior.
Members Using Keep My Studio
If you enable Keep My Studio, your Studio may be securely stored in the cloud to support backup, synchronization, and restoration.
You may request deletion of your cloud-stored Studio at any time.
What Is Deleted
When your Studio is deleted, we will remove your associated cloud data, including:
Wardrobe items
Photos
Collections
Favorites
Wish List
Saved Looks
Fragrance Library
Related Studio information
Processing Time
Deletion requests are processed as promptly as reasonably possible.
Some encrypted backups or disaster recovery systems may require additional time before information is permanently removed.
Legal Exceptions
Certain information may be retained where required to:
Comply with applicable laws.
Protect against fraud or abuse.
Resolve disputes.
Enforce legal agreements.
Contact
Questions or deletion requests may be sent to:
privacy@thestudiowardrobe.app
