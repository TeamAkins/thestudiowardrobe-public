# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026

Welcome to **The Studio**.

Your privacy is important to us.

This Privacy Policy explains what information The Studio collects, why it is collected, how it is used, and the choices you have regarding your information.

At The Studio, we believe privacy is part of good design.

Your wardrobe is personal. Your Studio is personal. The information you choose to add should remain under your control.

By using The Studio, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

# Our Privacy Philosophy

The Studio is built around a simple principle:

**Collect only the information necessary to provide the features you choose to use.**

Whenever possible, your information remains on your own Apple devices.

If you choose to enable **Keep My Studio**, Apple CloudKit is used to synchronize your Studio across your own connected Apple devices.

---

# Information You Choose to Add

You decide what becomes part of your Studio.

Depending on how you use the application, you may choose to add:

- Clothing
- Shoes
- Handbags
- Accessories
- Fragrances
- Clothing photographs
- Categories
- Brands
- Colors
- Seasons
- Occasions
- Care instructions
- Purchase information
- Wear history
- Favorites
- Wish List items
- Saved Looks
- Personal notes

You remain in control of the information you choose to add.

---

# Keep My Studio

Creating an account is optional.

You may use The Studio without enabling **Keep My Studio**.

If you choose to enable **Keep My Studio**, Apple CloudKit securely synchronizes your Studio across your supported Apple devices.

The Studio does not use CloudKit to create a public profile or share your information with other members.

---

# Camera

If you choose to photograph clothing or accessories, The Studio will request permission to access your device's camera.

Camera access is used only to capture photos that you choose to add to your Studio.

---

# Photos

If you choose to import existing images, The Studio will request permission to access your photo library.

Only the photos you select are imported.

The Studio does not browse or upload your photo library without your action.

---

# Microphone

If you choose to use Voice Entry, The Studio will request permission to access your microphone.

Microphone access is used only while you are actively using Voice Entry.

---

# Notifications

If you enable notifications, The Studio may send reminders or important application updates.

You may change notification preferences at any time through iOS Settings.

---

# Analytics

The Studio may collect limited diagnostic information to improve reliability, performance, and the overall member experience.

Analytics are never used to build advertising profiles or sell your personal information.

Where available, analytics may be disabled.

---

# Sharing

The Studio does not automatically share your wardrobe or personal information.

If future sharing features are introduced, only the content you explicitly choose to share will be shared.

---

# Removing Your Information

You remain in control of your Studio.

You may remove individual wardrobe items, photographs, Saved Looks, Favorites, Wish List items, notes, and other content at any time within the application.

If **Keep My Studio** is enabled, those changes are synchronized through Apple CloudKit to your other connected Apple devices.

Because The Studio does not maintain an independent copy of your Studio outside of Apple's CloudKit service, there is no separate "Delete My Data" request process.

---

# Children's Privacy

The Studio is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child in violation of applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to remove that information.
