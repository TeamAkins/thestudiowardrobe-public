# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026

Welcome to **The Studio**.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

We believe privacy is part of good design.

Your wardrobe is personal. Your Studio is personal. The information you choose to add should remain under your control.

By using The Studio, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

# Our Privacy Philosophy

The Studio was designed around a simple principle:

**Collect only what is necessary to provide the features you choose to use.**

We do not collect information simply because we can.

Whenever possible, your information remains on your device unless you choose to enable features that require cloud storage.

---

# Information You Choose to Provide

You decide what becomes part of your Studio.

Depending on how you use the application, you may choose to add:

* Clothing information
* Clothing photographs
* Shoes
* Handbags
* Accessories
* Fragrances
* Categories
* Colors
* Brands
* Seasons
* Occasions
* Care instructions
* Purchase information
* Wear history
* Saved Looks
* Favorites
* Wish List items
* Personal notes

You control the information you add and may edit or remove it at any time.

---

# Keep My Studio

Creating an account is optional.

You may enjoy The Studio without enabling **Keep My Studio**.

If you choose to enable **Keep My Studio**, we collect only the information necessary to provide features such as:

* Secure cloud backup
* Syncing across supported devices
* Restoring your Studio after replacing a device
* Future sharing features that you explicitly choose to use

Only members who enable **Keep My Studio** have Studio information stored in the cloud.

---

# Camera Access

If you choose to photograph clothing or accessories, The Studio will request permission to use your device's camera.

Camera access is used only to capture photos that you choose to add to your Studio.

---

# Photo Library Access

If you choose to import existing photos, The Studio will request access to your photo library.

Only the images you select are imported.

The Studio does not browse or upload your photo library without your action.

---

# Microphone Access

If you choose to use Voice Entry, The Studio will request access to your microphone.

Microphone access is used only while you are actively using Voice Entry.

---

# Notifications

If you enable notifications, The Studio may send reminders or updates, including:

* Outfit reminders
* Wish List reminders
* Important application updates

You can change notification preferences at any time in iOS Settings.

---

# Analytics

To improve reliability and performance, The Studio may collect limited diagnostic and usage information.

Where analytics are used, they are intended to help us understand how the application is performing, identify technical issues, and improve the overall experience.

We do not use analytics to build advertising profiles or sell your personal information.

Where supported, you may choose to disable analytics.

---

# Sharing

The Studio does not automatically share your wardrobe or personal information.

If future sharing features are available, only the collections, Looks, or other content that you explicitly choose to share will be shared.

You remain in control of what is shared and with whom.

---

# Data Storage

If you do not enable **Keep My Studio**, your Studio remains stored locally on your device.

If you enable **Keep My Studio**, your Studio information is securely stored to provide backup, synchronization, and restoration across your supported devices.

---

# Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide the services you have chosen to use or as required by applicable law.

If you request deletion of your Studio, we will remove your cloud-stored information in accordance with our Data Deletion Policy.

Some encrypted backups or disaster recovery systems may require additional time before information is permanently removed.

---

# Your Choices

You remain in control of your Studio.

You may:

* Edit your information
* Delete individual items
* Remove photographs
* Disable notifications
* Disable analytics where available
* Delete your Studio
* Contact us with privacy questions

---

# Children's Privacy

The Studio is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child in violation of applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to remove that information.

---

# Changes to This Privacy Policy

As The Studio evolves, we may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When significant changes are made, we will update the Effective Date and, where appropriate, notify members within the application.

The most current version of this Privacy Policy will always be available on our public website.
