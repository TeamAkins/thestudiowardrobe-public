# accessibility-statement.md

# Accessibility Statement

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026

## Our Commitment

At The Studio, accessibility is part of thoughtful design.

We believe beautiful experiences should also be inclusive experiences. Every member should feel welcome, supported, and able to enjoy The Studio with confidence.

Accessibility is not a feature added at the end of development—it is an ongoing commitment that informs how we design, build, and improve the application.

---

## Accessibility Features

The Studio is designed to support accessibility technologies and system features available on Apple platforms, including:

* Dynamic Type
* VoiceOver
* High Contrast
* Reduce Motion
* Large touch targets
* Clear navigation
* Meaningful accessibility labels
* Keyboard navigation where supported

As Apple introduces new accessibility capabilities, we will evaluate and adopt them where appropriate.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing process.

We regularly review and improve The Studio to provide a better experience for everyone.

---

## Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier or have suggestions that would improve your experience, we'd love to hear from you.

Please contact us at:

accessibility**@thestudiowardrobe.app**

Your feedback helps us create a more welcoming Studio for everyone.

# studio-principles.md

# Studio Principles

The Studio is built on a simple belief:

**Beautiful design should quietly improve everyday life.**

Everything we create is guided by a few enduring principles.

---

## Technology Should Quietly Disappear

Technology is at its best when it feels effortless.

Our goal is to help you focus on your wardrobe—not the interface.

---

## Create, Don't Consume

The Studio encourages thoughtful choices over constant accumulation.

We believe creativity begins with appreciating what you already own.

---

## Beauty with Purpose

Every screen, interaction, and detail should earn its place.

We value simplicity, clarity, and craftsmanship over complexity.

---

## Your Studio Belongs to You

Your wardrobe reflects your story.

Your Studio should remain personal, private, and entirely under your control.

---

## Thoughtful Organization Creates Clarity

Organization isn't about perfection.

It's about making everyday decisions feel calmer, simpler, and more intentional.

---

## Designed to Grow With You

A Studio is never truly finished.

It evolves as your style evolves, one thoughtfully chosen piece at a time.

---

## Welcome

Thank you for making The Studio part of your everyday life.

We hope it becomes more than an app.

We hope it becomes a place you'll enjoy returning to—one that inspires you to curate thoughtfully, create beautifully, and express yourself with confidence.
